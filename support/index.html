Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@notegym.app and we’ll get back to you.

How do I make it easier or harder?

Tap Settings. You can change the rhythms, the range of notes, the key and the time signature. Change the tempo on the main screen. If the music feels easy, change one thing at a time.

I can’t hear the click.

Check that Click is turned on, on the main screen or in Settings, and turn up the volume. NoteGym plays even when your iPhone is set to silent.

Why did the music stop?

Calls, Siri, alarms, other apps playing sound and unplugging headphones all pause NoteGym. Tap Play to carry on where you left off.

Which clefs can I read?

Guitar (treble clef, sounding an octave lower), bass clef and treble clef. You can also practise rhythm on its own.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
