Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

NoteGym doesn’t collect any information about you.

What stays on your iPhone

NoteGym saves your settings, such as tempo, clef, rhythms and note range, on your iPhone so they’re there next time you open the app. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

What NoteGym doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t connect to the internet.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, ads or analytics.
  • It doesn’t track you or share anything with anyone.
  • It doesn’t use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

Purchases

You buy NoteGym through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we receive no payment details from it. Apple’s own privacy policy covers that purchase.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don’t share them.

Children

NoteGym collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@notegym.app.
